Version: 1.0

# Overview

Adepa Pork Hub is the official e-commerce website for fresh and processed pork products produced by Symas Farms.

This website focuses entirely on selling premium pork products directly to consumers, restaurants, hotels, caterers, retailers, and wholesalers.

The website should prominently mention that all pork products are sourced from Symas Farms.
